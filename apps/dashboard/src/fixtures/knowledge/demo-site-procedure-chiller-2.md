# Demo site procedure: Chiller 2 condenser tube cleaning

This procedure removes condenser tube fouling on Chiller 2.

> Synthetic demonstration procedure for the dashboard's demo plant (3 chillers, 4 cooling towers, 4 condenser water and 4 chilled water pumps). It is not a real site's procedure.

## Scope

CW pump 2 feeds Chiller 2, and the condenser water from Chiller 2 returns to the cooling towers through the common header. Chiller 1 and Chiller 3 carry the load while Chiller 2 is out of service.

## Isolation steps

1. Confirm that Chiller 1 and Chiller 3 can carry the cooling load at the current wet-bulb temperature.
2. Stage Chiller 2 off from the plant controls and wait for its compressor to stop.
3. Stop CW pump 2, then close the condenser isolation valves of Chiller 2.
4. Close the chilled water isolation valves of Chiller 2 and stop CHW pump 2 if no other chiller needs it.
5. Lock out the motor of CW pump 2 before the water boxes are opened.

## Cleaning and inspection

Brush and flush the condenser tubes. Note any scale deposits or tube damage, because scale buildup returns quickly when water treatment is poor.

## Return to service

Refill and vent the condenser and open its isolation valves. Start CW pump 2 and confirm the flow at the flow meter before staging Chiller 2 back on. Record the condenser approach at a comparable load; a clean condenser shows an approach close to its commissioning value.
