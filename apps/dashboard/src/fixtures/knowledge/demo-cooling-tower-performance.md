# Demo cooling tower performance notes

> Synthetic demonstration notes for the dashboard's demo mode. They are not a manufacturer's data or a standard.

## Approach and range

The tower approach is the difference between the cold water leaving the tower and the wet-bulb temperature of the entering air. The cooling range is the difference between the hot water entering the tower and the cold water leaving it. At a fixed load, a rising approach means the tower is rejecting heat less effectively.

## Causes of poor tower performance

Fill fouling and scale buildup reduce the wetted surface of the fill media, which raises the approach and causes high condenser water temperature.

A fan failure on one cell reduces the airflow, so the remaining cells cannot hold the condenser water setpoint on a hot day.

Low flow through a cell, for example from blocked spray nozzles, leaves dry patches in the fill and raises the leaving water temperature.

Biological growth fouls the fill and the basin and is a health risk, so water treatment and blowdown are kept under control.

## Inspection checklist

1. Check each fan, gearbox and motor for vibration and abnormal noise.
2. Inspect the drift eliminator for damage; damaged eliminators increase drift loss.
3. Check the spray nozzles and the fill media for fouling and scale deposits.
4. Confirm the basin level and the make-up valve; a low basin level can starve the condenser water pumps.

## Plant notes

Cooling tower 1 to Cooling tower 4 share a common condenser water header. Cooling tower 4 serves the header only when its fan is available. Where a contract requires a thermal performance acceptance test, it follows CTI ATC-105.
