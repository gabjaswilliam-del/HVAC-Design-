# Demo pump troubleshooting guide

> Synthetic demonstration guide for the dashboard's demo mode, covering the condenser water and chilled water pumps. It is not a manufacturer manual.

## Cavitation

Cavitation is the formation and collapse of vapour bubbles at the impeller when the pressure at the pump suction falls too low. It sounds like gravel passing through the pump. Cavitation causes vibration and, over time, erodes the impeller. A clogged strainer on the suction side is a common cause, because it adds pressure drop before the pump.

## Bearing wear

Bearing wear shows as rising vibration and a higher bearing temperature. Shaft misalignment between the motor and the pump increases the bearing load and leads to bearing wear, so check the coupling alignment after any motor work.

## Seal leaks

A mechanical seal leak shows as water at the seal gland. Running the pump dry, or with abrasive water, causes seal failure.

## Standby pump rotation procedure

This procedure prevents bearing wear and stuck valves on standby pumps that stand idle for weeks.

1. Run CW pump 4, the standby condenser water pump, for one hour each week and log its vibration and motor current.
2. Run CHW pump 4, the standby chilled water pump, on the same day.
3. Exercise the isolation valve of each standby pump during the run.
