# Demo reference card: data centre cooling terms

> Synthetic demonstration reference for the dashboard's demo mode. It summarises general terms and names standards without quoting them.

## Efficiency terms

Power usage effectiveness (PUE) is the total facility energy divided by the energy of the IT equipment; ISO/IEC 30134-2 defines it. ASHRAE 90.4 sets energy requirements for data centres.

## Temperatures and differences

Delta-T is the difference between the return and supply water temperatures. A low delta-T means more water is pumped for the same cooling load.

## Redundancy

N+1 redundancy keeps one more chiller, pump or cooling tower available than the load needs, so one unit can be serviced, or can fail, without losing cooling.
